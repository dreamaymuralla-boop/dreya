About Mobile Legends: Bang Bang

Mobile Legends: Bang Bang is a multiplayer online battle arena (MOBA) game for mobile devices. Players compete in teams and select heroes with different abilities and roles.

The six main hero roles are Tank, Fighter, Assassin, Mage, Marksman, and Support. Each role contributes differently to the team. Players use teamwork, strategy, and map awareness to destroy the opposing team's base.

Every match offers opportunities to improve skills, coordinate with teammates, and experience exciting battles.
