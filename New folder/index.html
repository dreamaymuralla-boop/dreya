About MLBB: Bang Bang

Mobile Legends: Bang Bang is a multiplayer online battle arena (MOBA) game designed for smartphones and tablets.

The game is free-to-play and includes optional in-game purchases such as heroes and skins. Each player controls a selectable character called a Hero, with unique abilities and traits.

There are six main hero roles: Tank, Marksman, Assassin, Fighter, Mage, and Support. Players can customize their builds using in-game items and emblems.
